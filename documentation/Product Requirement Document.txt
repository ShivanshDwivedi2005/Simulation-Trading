SIMTRADE — PRODUCT REQUIREMENT DOCUMENT
Version: 0.1
Status: Initial vertical slice

1. Product objective
Deliver a fast, credible US-equity paper-trading experience in which a registered user can discover an instrument, inspect OHLC market data, place an internally simulated order, and immediately understand the impact on cash, positions, and P&L.

2. Primary persona
An aspiring or practicing trader who understands basic order terminology and wants realistic practice with no real-money exposure.

3. Initial success flow
Register -> verify email OTP -> sign in -> receive $100,000 virtual cash -> search AAPL -> review historical and live-style prices -> submit BUY order -> receive fill based on the shared quote source -> see position, cash, and P&L update.

4. V0.1 scope
Authentication
- Manual registration with name, email, password, and OTP verification.
- Google OAuth.
- Secure access/refresh token lifecycle and logout.
- Argon2id or bcrypt password hashing; no plaintext password storage.

Trading account
- One default USD simulation account with $100,000 initial cash.
- Cash, buying power, equity, positions, realized/unrealized/total P&L.
- Order and trade history.

Market experience
- Start with 20–30 liquid US equities; initial interface demonstrates AAPL, NVDA, MSFT, GOOGL, and AMZN.
- Search, watchlist, quote, bid/ask, spread, volume, OHLC candles, and timeframes.
- Historical data over REST and live updates over WebSocket.
- Chart and execution engine must use the same normalized market event.

Order experience
- BUY and SELL MARKET, LIMIT, STOP, and STOP_LIMIT orders.
- States: NEW, ACCEPTED, PARTIALLY_FILLED, FILLED, CANCELLED, REJECTED.
- Market buys use simulated ask; market sells use simulated bid.
- Clear pre-submit estimate, rejection reason, fill confirmation, and cancellable open orders.

Risk and monitoring
- Sufficient buying power, max order quantity, max position size, concentration, max daily loss, instrument status, and duplicate validation.
- Configurable limits.
- Educational compliance alerts for cancellation rate, trading frequency, wash-like behavior, spoofing-like behavior, concentration, restricted symbols, rate violations, and abnormal size.

5. Deferred after stable vertical slice
- Iceberg replenishment behavior.
- Generalized multi-leg strategies: outright, spread, butterfly, double butterfly.
- Futures, commodities, forex, crypto, India, and UK markets.
- Depth-aware partial fills, liquidity, slippage curves, latency, and market impact.
- Instructor/team workflows, leaderboards, and payments.

6. Functional acceptance criteria
- A new verified user has exactly $100,000 virtual cash.
- Selecting a watchlist symbol updates the quote, chart, and order ticket together.
- A valid market BUY fills at the current normalized ask and atomically updates order, trade, cash, and position data.
- An order exceeding available buying power is rejected with an actionable reason.
- Accepted non-market orders appear in order history and can be cancelled.
- Reconnecting a browser restores authoritative server state; browser state is not the ledger.
- Thousands of watchers for one symbol share one upstream subscription.

7. Experience acceptance criteria
- Core trading action is visible in the first desktop viewport.
- All controls are keyboard reachable with visible focus.
- Functional states use text or shape in addition to color.
- The interface works without horizontal page scrolling at 375, 768, 1024, and 1440 px.
- Motion respects prefers-reduced-motion.
- Charts expose a text summary and tabular fallback.

8. Product metrics
- Registration-to-first-fill conversion.
- Median time from verified account to first filled order.
- Order rejection rate by reason.
- Weekly returning traders and completed learning sessions.
- Market event-to-browser and order-to-fill latency percentiles.
- Position/P&L reconciliation error count (target: zero).
